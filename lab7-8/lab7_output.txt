1. Advantage of Log-Space: Completely eliminates scale factor arrays and division-by-zero risks.
2. Advantage of Scaling: Avoids expensive transcendental log/exp calls; scaled forward alpha_hat

--- Task x: Theoretical Comparison & Impossible Sequence Construction ---
1. Optimisation Objective Difference:
   - Posterior decoding maximises the expected number of individual correct states (pointwise error).
   - Viterbi decoding maximises the joint trajectory probability of the entire chain.
2. Why Posterior Decoding Can Return a Sequence with Probability Zero:
   - Suppose transitions S_1 -> S_1 and S_2 -> S_2 have probability EXACTLY 0.0.
   - If independent marginals at t=1 and t=2 both favour S_1 (e.g. P=0.51),
     posterior decoding outputs (S_1, S_1).
   - But A_{S_1, S_1} = 0, so P((S_1, S_1), Y) = 0.0 (an impossible physical state path!).
   - Viterbi enforces global transition consistency and will never choose an impossible path.
